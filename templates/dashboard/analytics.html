Model Transparency

This system predicts next-day traffic congestion and volume. The model was trained on daily-granularity data with a t+1 day horizon — it forecasts tomorrow's conditions, not the next 15 minutes.

Classification accuracy of 73.08% means that roughly 3 in 4 intersection-day predictions correctly identify the congestion class (low/moderate/high/severe). The Macro-F1 of 0.6858 is reported alongside accuracy because it accounts for class imbalance — the dataset has more "severe" days than "low" days. Balanced accuracy of 67.47% shows performance across all classes equally weighted.

Regression MAE of 5,200 vehicles means the volume prediction is off by about 5,200 vehicles on average. The MAPE of 23.84% and R² of 0.588 indicate moderate predictive power — useful for planning and decision support, not precise traffic control.
